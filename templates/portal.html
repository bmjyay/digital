How it works

From sign-up to your host's front door. Three steps, mostly on us.

The organizers handle the matching. All you need to do is sign up, then check your email or the portal once assignments go out.

  1. 01

    Sign up

    Attendees say they need a place to stay when they register. Yale students who want to host sign up with their Yale email.

  2. 02

    We make the matches

    Organizers run the matcher. It pairs guests with hosts of the same gender, keeps any pairings you set up ahead of time, and respects each host's space.

  3. 03

    Meet your host

    Assignments go out by email, and hosts can log in here to see who's staying with them. Reach out, sort out arrival times, and you're set.

About IMC

Why this portal exists.

Every year since 2009, the Chaplain's Office, in collaboration with the Yale Muslim Students Association, hosts the Ivy Muslim Conference (IMC), an overnight gathering for Muslim students from the Ivy League and beyond.

As attendance has grown to 300+ students from 20+ schools, the most time-consuming task for organizers has become housing: matching attendees to hosts under capacity constraints, honoring pre-arranged matches, and emailing assignments manually.

The IMC Conference Housing Portal automates this process so organizers can spend less time in spreadsheets and more time building a meaningful experience for attendees.

FAQs

Good questions.

The things people ask us most often.

Who is the portal for?

IMC attendees who need somewhere to stay, and Yale students who are hosting them. Organizers use the admin side to run the matching.

How are guests matched with hosts?

Guests are paired with hosts of the same gender, and nobody is given more guests than they said they can take. If you've already arranged to stay with someone, tell the organizers and we'll keep that pairing.

Does it cost anything?

No. Hosts volunteer their space, and there's no fee to stay with one.

When will I find out where I'm staying?

Organizers send assignments by email once matching is done, usually in the days before the conference. Hosts can also log in here to see their guests' names and contact details.

Can I stay with a friend?

Yes. If a Yale student has already agreed to host you, let the organizers know before matching and we'll lock it in.
